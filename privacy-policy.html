Privacy Policy for Tap & Talk

Last updated: September 6, 2026

Tap & Talk ("we," "us," or "our") is a mobile app created by Stronger Daughters LLC that helps moms and daughters have meaningful conversations and save those answers as a keepsake over time. We care deeply about your privacy and your family's privacy. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

If you have any questions, reach us at hello@strongerdaughters.com.

Who This App Is For

Tap & Talk is designed for parents and guardians to use with their daughters. The account holder is an adult. Daughters do not create their own accounts, log in, or interact with the app independently. All information about a daughter is entered and controlled by the parent or guardian who owns the account.

What We Collect

Account information. When you create an account, we collect your email address and name so we can sign you in and keep your keepsake safe across devices.

Daughter profiles. You can create one or more profiles for your daughters. For each profile, you may enter a name (or nickname) and an age. You control this information entirely, and you can edit or delete it at any time.

Answers you log. When you record an answer to a conversation card, we save that answer, along with the date and the age you entered, so your keepsake builds over time. These answers belong to you.

Subscription status. If you subscribe, our payment partner (RevenueCat, working with Apple) tells us whether your subscription is active, so we can unlock the app for you. We do not see or store your credit card or payment details, those are handled entirely by Apple.

Device information. Like most apps, we automatically receive basic technical information such as your device type, operating system version, and app version, which helps us keep the app working and fix problems.

What We Do Not Collect

We do not collect precise location data. We do not sell your information. We do not run advertising in the app, and we do not share your family's answers with advertisers. We do not require a daughter to provide any information directly to us.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information above only to:

  • Create and secure your account
  • Save and display your conversation answers as a keepsake over time
  • Let you manage multiple daughter profiles under one subscription
  • Confirm your subscription so the app unlocks
  • Keep the app running, fix bugs, and improve the experience

How Information Is Stored

Your account and keepsake data are stored securely using our hosting partner, Supabase, which provides encrypted, industry-standard database infrastructure. Subscription status is managed through RevenueCat. We take reasonable measures to protect your information, though no method of storage or transmission over the internet is ever completely secure.

Sharing Content

Tap & Talk includes a "Share this moment" feature that lets you create an image to share (for example, to Instagram) if you choose to. You are always in control of what is shared. You can hide your daughter's answer before sharing, and we never include your daughter's name or age in a shared image. Nothing is ever shared automatically, sharing only happens when you tap the button and choose to post.

Children's Privacy

Because a daughter's name, age, and answers may be entered by the parent, we treat this information with special care. Tap & Talk is intended to be used by an adult parent or guardian, not by a child directly. We do not knowingly allow children to create accounts or provide information to us on their own. A parent may review, edit, or delete any daughter profile and its saved answers at any time from within the app, or by contacting us at hello@strongerdaughters.com. If you believe a child has provided us information without a parent's involvement, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your Choices and Rights

You can, at any time:

  • Edit or delete any daughter profile and its saved answers
  • Request a copy of your data by emailing us
  • Request deletion of your entire account and all associated data by emailing us at hello@strongerdaughters.com

Data Retention

We keep your keepsake data for as long as your account is active, because the whole point of Tap & Talk is to preserve those answers over the years. If you delete your account, we delete your associated data.

Changes to This Policy

If we make meaningful changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app. Continued use of Tap & Talk after changes means you accept the updated policy.
